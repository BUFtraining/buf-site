Common Questions

Hell's Kitchenpersonal training FAQ.

How much does a personal trainer in Hell's Kitchen cost?

At BUF, every session is under $100 — well below the Midtown average of $130–190. 1-on-1 training runs $90–98 per session depending on how often you train, and semi-private (1-on-2) sessions are even less per person. No contracts, no membership fees.

Where is the studio located?

We're at 347 W 36th St, Suite 1002-1004, New York, NY 10018 — in the heart of Hell's Kitchen and Midtown West, a short walk from Penn Station, Hudson Yards, and the Garment District.

Do I need a gym membership to train here?

No. BUF is a private personal training studio, not a gym. You pay only for the sessions you book — no membership, no contract, no sign-up fees.

What kinds of training do you offer?

1-on-1 personal training, semi-private (1-on-2) training, standalone mobility sessions, virtual training, and diet coaching. Every program is built around your goals — fat loss, strength, mobility, or general fitness.

Are your trainers certified?

Yes. Our trainers hold nationally recognized certifications (NASM, ACE, NSCA) and have helped over 1,000 New Yorkers reach their goals since 2017. BUF holds a 5-star Google rating. Meet the BUF team.
