Who It's For

For anyone whose goalis decided in the kitchen.

If you're training for fat loss, muscle gain, or just to feel better, what you eat determines at least half the outcome. We don't think that should be a separate $200-a-month add-on, so nutrition coaching is included with any 1-on-1 or semi-private training plan at BUF.

Ben Unger, BUF's founder and a NASM Certified Nutrition Coach, handles nutrition consultations personally. The approach is simple on purpose: protein first, a realistic calorie target, food you actually like, and habits that survive a New York work week.

What it isn't: a meal plan you'll abandon in nine days, a supplement stack, or a cleanse. And it isn't medical nutrition therapy — if you have a diagnosed condition that affects your diet, we'll work alongside your physician or a registered dietitian, not in place of them.

How It Works

What nutrition coachingincludes.

01

Nutrition consultation

A conversation with Ben about how you currently eat, your schedule, what you've tried before, and what your training goal requires.

02

Your targets

A daily protein target and a calorie range matched to your goal — set high enough to support training and low enough to move the scale in the right direction, if that's the aim.

03

Food logging in the BUF app

Clients on nutrition coaching can log meals in the BUF client app, including barcode scanning and a library of common NYC lunch spots, so your trainer can see what's actually happening between sessions.

04

Check-ins with your trainer

Your coach reviews your log and progress alongside your training. Adjustments are small and frequent rather than dramatic.

05

Realistic, not perfect

We plan for the client dinner, the weekend, and the week you're slammed. Consistency over 12 weeks beats perfection over 12 days.

Common Questions

Nutrition coachingFAQ.

Is nutrition coaching really free?

Yes. It's included with any BUF training plan — 1-on-1 or semi-private. We don't offer it as a standalone paid service.

Will I get a meal plan?

No. Rigid meal plans fail fast. You'll get protein and calorie targets, guidance on building meals you like, and coaching on the habits that actually move the needle.

Who does the nutrition coaching?

Ben Unger, BUF's founder and a NASM Certified Nutrition Coach, runs the consultations. Your trainer supports the day-to-day check-ins.

Do I have to log my food?

It helps a lot, but it's your call. Clients who log in the BUF app get more precise coaching because the trainer can see real intake rather than estimates.

Can you help with a medical diet or eating disorder recovery?

That's outside what a nutrition coach should handle. If you have a diagnosed condition or a clinical history that affects your eating, we'll coordinate with your physician or a registered dietitian rather than replace them.

I just want nutrition help, not training. Can I sign up?

Not at the moment — nutrition coaching is bundled with training. If you're on the fence about training, book a free consultation and we'll talk through what fits.
